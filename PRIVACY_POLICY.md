# Privacy Policy

**Muay Thai Timer**  
**Effective date:** June 28, 2026  
**Last updated:** June 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how **Muay Thai Timer** ("the App," "we," "us," or "our") handles information when you use our mobile application on Android devices.

Muay Thai Timer is a training interval timer for Muay Thai and similar workouts. The App is designed to work **entirely on your device** and does **not** require an account, internet connection, or registration to use its core features.

---

## 1. Summary

- We do **not** collect, sell, or share your personal information.
- We do **not** use analytics, advertising, or tracking services.
- Exercise names, durations, and rest settings you enter are kept **only in the App's memory** while it is running and are **not saved** to your device or sent to any server.
- The App may write **temporary audio files** to your device's local cache to play timer sounds.
- The App does **not** access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos, or files outside of its own cache.

---

## 2. Information We Do Not Collect

Muay Thai Timer does **not** collect:

- Name, email address, phone number, or other contact details
- Account or login credentials
- Location data
- Photos, videos, or other media from your device
- Contacts or calendar data
- Health or fitness data stored outside the App
- Device identifiers for advertising or tracking
- Payment or billing information

The App does not include in-app purchases or subscriptions at this time.

---

## 3. Information Processed Locally on Your Device

When you use the App, the following information is processed **only on your device** and **only while the App is open**:

| Data | Purpose | Stored? | Transmitted? |
|------|---------|---------|--------------|
| Exercise names you enter | Display and run your workout timer | No (memory only) | No |
| Exercise durations | Run your workout timer | No (memory only) | No |
| Rest time setting | Run rest intervals between exercises | No (memory only) | No |
| Timer state (current phase, remaining time) | Operate the timer during a session | No (memory only) | No |

When you close or force-stop the App, this information is discarded. We do not persist it to local storage or sync it to the cloud.

---

## 4. Local Cache (Audio Files)

The App plays sound cues (such as a start bell and end-of-round tone) to help you track intervals. To play certain sounds, the App may temporarily create small audio files in your device's **application cache directory**.

These files:

- Are generated and used only for sound playback
- Remain on your device and are not uploaded anywhere
- May be cleared automatically by your device when storage is low or when you clear the App's cache

We do not access your personal music, recordings, or other files on your device.

---

## 5. Permissions

Muay Thai Timer may request or use device capabilities needed for its core functionality, such as **audio playback**. The App does not record audio from your microphone.

The App does **not** require network access for its core timer features and does not intentionally send data over the internet.

If Android permissions change in a future update, this policy will be updated to reflect those changes.

---

## 6. Third-Party Services

Muay Thai Timer does **not** integrate third-party analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or social login services.

The App is distributed through **Google Play**. Google may collect information about your download, device, and use of the store according to [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). That collection is governed by Google, not by us.

---

## 7. Children's Privacy

Muay Thai Timer is suitable for general audiences and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Because we do not collect personal data, no parental consent is required for data collection through the App itself.

If you believe we have inadvertently received personal information from a child, please contact us using the details below and we will address your request.

---

## 8. Data Security

Because personal data is not collected or transmitted by the App, there is no central database of user information to secure. Information you enter during a session exists only in the App's runtime memory on your device.

We recommend keeping your device updated and protected with a screen lock to help safeguard any apps you use.

---

## 9. Your Choices and Rights

You can:

- **Stop using the App** at any time
- **Uninstall the App** to remove it and its local cache from your device
- **Clear the App's cache** through your Android device settings

Depending on your location, you may have rights under privacy laws (such as GDPR or CCPA). Because we do not collect personal information, most data-subject requests (access, correction, deletion of personal data held by us) are not applicable. If you have questions about your rights, contact us using the information below.
